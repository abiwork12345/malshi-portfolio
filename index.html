Comfortable in front of a lens, clear behind a mic.

Malshi Edirirathne is a presenter, model and voice artist from Sri Lanka. She works with NCG Holdings, where she fronts the group's service films, and she has presented and modeled for Lumen Optical, voiced the Kalathea Villa Hikkaduwa promo, and worked on shoots with the digital agency Nexteye Solutions.

Her work runs from a Sinhala-language film for a bus and lorry workshop to eyewear reels and a couple shoot at Galle Fort. On camera she is warm and unhurried, and she delivers a script so it sounds like conversation. Behind the mic she keeps the same clarity. Brands book her because viewers stay and watch.
